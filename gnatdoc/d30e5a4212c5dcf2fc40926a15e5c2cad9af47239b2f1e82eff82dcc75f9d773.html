Configure

procedure Configure
   (Timeout       : Milliseconds;
    Debug_Pause   : Boolean := True)

Only Proc0 and Proc1 are reset when the watchdog is triggered. Clocks, SRAM, and buses are unaffected by the watchdog. (TODO: PSM_WDSEL config)

When Debug_Pause is True, the Watchdog timer does not run as long as a debugger is attached.
